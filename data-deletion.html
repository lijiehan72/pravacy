Account & Data Deletion

Application: Bangkok Driver · Operated by Beijing Zeshan Information Technology Co., Ltd. (北京泽杉信息科技有限公司) · Last updated: September 30, 2025

This page explains how Bangkok Driver users can delete their account, and what happens to their data. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

1. How to delete your account

a. Delete directly in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Bangkok Driver app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (the "我的" tab in the bottom navigation bar).
  3. In the Legal section, below Terms of Service, tap Delete Account (注销账号), shown in red.
  4. Read the confirmation notice and tap Delete Account again to confirm.
  5. You will be signed out immediately and returned to the login screen. A confirmation message will be shown.

b. Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us at chenglong@theshine.com.cn from the phone number / email associated with your account, or call +86 18611430143. We will verify your identity and process the request within the timeframe below.

2. What happens to your data

a. Deleted immediately upon request

  • Your phone number, full name, password and login credentials.
  • Your driver profile and qualification information.
  • Uploaded photographs and documents (ID card, driver license, vehicle photos, insurance).
  • Your active login sessions are revoked; your existing access token stops working immediately.

All remaining personal data associated with your account will be completely erased within 7 business days.

b. Retained in anonymized form

To comply with accounting, tax and legal record-keeping obligations, the following records are retained in an anonymized form only, with all fields linking them to you removed:

  • Past trip/order records and financial ledger entries.

These anonymized records cannot be used to identify you or reconstruct your account.

3. Before you delete

  • Outstanding balance: If your driver wallet still holds a balance, please withdraw it first. Account deletion cannot be completed while a balance remains.
  • Pending applications: Drivers whose onboarding application is still under review may also delete their account; the pending application record will be removed as well.
