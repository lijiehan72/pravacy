Bangkok Driver — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2025

Bangkok Driver ("we", "us", or "our") operates a driver-side mobile application that connects professional drivers with ride requests in the Bangkok metropolitan area. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information you provide directly

  • Account credentials: Phone number and password used to sign up and sign in.
  • Driver profile: Full name, license number, vehicle plate number, vehicle model, and other details required for qualification review.
  • Photographs: Driver license, ID card, vehicle registration, and other supporting documents uploaded for verification.
  • Profile updates: Any changes you submit to your driver or vehicle information.

b. Information collected automatically

  • Precise location data: When you go online to accept trip requests, your device sends real-time GPS coordinates so we can match you with nearby passengers and show your arrival. Location continues to be collected during active trips.
  • Device information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifier, app version, and network status.
  • Audio recordings: With your explicit permission, short audio recordings may be captured in rare cases to resolve serious trip-related disputes (e.g., safety incidents). These are never shared with third parties unless required by law.

2. How We Use Information

  • Authenticate your account and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Review your driver qualification and approve your application.
  • Match you with nearby trip requests and dispatch rides.
  • Calculate fares, track trip progress, and generate earnings statements.
  • Improve our matching algorithms and driver experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • With passengers: Your first name, vehicle plate number, and live location are shown to the passenger you are serving.
  • With payment processors: Limited payment details (never your full banking credentials) are passed to secure payment gateways to process fare settlements.
  • With authorities: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or valid government request.

4. Data Storage & Retention

  • Data is stored on secure servers located in Southeast Asia.
  • We retain your account data for as long as your account is active, plus 24 months after account closure for tax and legal compliance.
  • Trip records are retained for 5 years to support dispute resolution and financial auditing.

5. Your Rights

  • Access: You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You can update or correct your profile at any time through the app.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us. We will delete within 30 days except where retention is legally required.
  • Withdraw consent: You can revoke location or audio permissions at any time via your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Bangkok Driver is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced via the app and posted here with a revised "Last updated" date.
